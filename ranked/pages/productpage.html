Product Page

The Product Page is one of the most important pages in your Shopify store, as it presents a single product along with all the essential information customers need before making a purchase. In the Ranked theme, the product page is designed to highlight your product with a clean layout, high-quality media, and well-structured details that help customers easily understand the product and make confident buying decisions.

This page includes a product media gallery, product title, vendor name, pricing details, SKU, and a detailed product description. Customers can also select product variants such as size or color, check inventory availability, adjust the quantity, and add the item to their cart directly from this page. Additional blocks such as pickup availability, trust badges, complementary products, collapsible information tabs, and social share buttons can be enabled to enhance the shopping experience and provide more useful information to your customers.

Configure product page Try the theme

Setup

How to Configure the Product Page

Steps
  • From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes.
  • Click Customize on the Ranked theme.
  • In the Theme Editor, use the page selector dropdown and choose Products → Default product.
  • From the left sidebar, select the Product section to access the product page settings.
  • Add, remove, or rearrange product blocks such as Title, Price, Variant Picker, Inventory, and Add to Cart according to your store’s needs.
  • Adjust the section settings and layout options available on the right panel.
  • Click Save to apply your changes.

Product Section Settings

The Product section settings control the overall visual styling, product media gallery layout, slider navigation arrows, split details arrangement, and responsive spacing for the product page template.

  • Color scheme: Choose from predefined theme color palettes (e.g., Scheme 1, Scheme 2, etc.) to style section background surfaces, card containers, and typography. Click Edit scheme to customize colors directly.
  • Show breadcrumb: Toggle on to display a breadcrumb navigation trail above the details, helping customers understand their location within the store hierarchy (e.g., Home / Category / Product).
  • Image gallery:
    • Layout: Choose how the product images are displayed (e.g., Image slider on top or Stack layout).
    • Image ratio: Choose the aspect ratio of the product images (e.g., Portrait or Square).
    • Image fit: Choose how the product images fit within their display area (e.g., Show full image or Fill the frame).
    • Image corner radius: Adjust the corner rounding of the product images (e.g., 28 px).
  • Sticky add to cart: Toggle on to display a sticky "Add to Cart" button as users scroll through the product details.
  • Details box:
    • Show details box: A bordered box below the product details. Choose Placement in a block's settings to move it into the box.
    • Left column width: Adjust the width of the left column in the product details box (e.g., 40%).
  • Edge shadows:
    • Show top shadow: Enable or disable the shadow at the top edge of the section.
    • Top shadow color scheme: Choose the color scheme used for the top shadow.
    • Top shadow height: Adjust the height of the top shadow (e.g., 180 px).
    • Show bottom shadow: Enable or disable the shadow at the bottom edge of the section.
  • Desktop padding:
    • Padding top: Adjust top vertical spacing on desktop screens (slider in pixels, e.g., 20 px).
    • Padding bottom: Adjust bottom vertical spacing on desktop screens (slider in pixels, e.g., 40 px).
  • Mobile padding:
    • Padding top: Adjust top vertical spacing on mobile devices (slider in pixels, e.g., 20 px).
    • Padding bottom: Adjust bottom vertical spacing on mobile devices (slider in pixels, e.g., 40 px).

Blocks

Available Product Block

  • Inventory: Displays real-time stock availability and inventory urgency badges on the product page (e.g., 9 LEFT, IN STOCK, SOLD OUT).
    • Width: Set the width of the inventory block as a percentage of the available row width (e.g., 20%). Blocks appear side by side until the row is full. Narrow blocks widen on tablets, and every block takes full width on mobile.
    • Show divider on the right: Enable to display a vertical divider on the right side of the block when blocks are placed side by side on desktop.
    • Content position: Align the inventory content to the Left, Center, or Right.
    • Placement: Choose where to display the inventory block: within Product details, in the Details box, left column, or in the Details box, right column.
  • Vendor: Displays the product's vendor name on the product page.
    • Width: Set the width of the vendor block as a percentage of the available row width (e.g., 25%). Blocks appear side by side until the row is full. Narrow blocks widen on tablets, and every block takes full width on mobile.
    • Show divider on the right: Enable to display a vertical divider on the right side of the block when blocks are placed side by side on desktop.
    • Content position: Align the vendor content to the Left, Center, or Right.
    • Placement: Choose where to display the vendor block: within Product details, in the Details box, left column, or in the Details box, right column.
  • Text: Displays custom text or dynamic content (e.g., Short Description) on the product page.
    • Text: Add custom text or select dynamic content to display in this block.
    • Width: Set the width of the text block as a percentage of the available row width (e.g., 30%). Blocks appear side by side until the row is full. Narrow blocks widen on tablets, and every block takes full width on mobile.
    • Show divider on the right: Enable to display a vertical divider on the right side of the block when blocks are placed side by side on desktop.
    • Content position: Align the text content to the Left, Center, or Right.
    • Placement: Choose where to display the text block: within Product details, in the Details box, left column, or in the Details box, right column.
  • Price: Displays the product price, including sale pricing when applicable.
    • Width: Set the width of the price block as a percentage of the available row width (e.g., 25%). Blocks appear side by side until the row is full. Narrow blocks widen on tablets, and every block takes full width on mobile.
    • Show divider on the right: Enable to display a vertical divider on the right side of the block when blocks are placed side by side on desktop.
    • Content position: Align the price content to the Left, Center, or Right.
    • Placement: Choose where to display the price block: within Product details, in the Details box, left column, or in the Details box, right column.
  • Title: Displays the product title on the product page.
    • Width: Set the width of the title block as a percentage of the available row width (e.g., 33%). Blocks appear side by side until the row is full. Narrow blocks widen on tablets, and every block takes full width on mobile.
    • Show divider on the right: Enable to display a vertical divider on the right side of the block when blocks are placed side by side on desktop.
    • Content position: Align the title content to the Left, Center, or Right.
    • Placement: Choose where to display the title block: within Product details, in the Details box, left column, or in the Details box, right column.
    • Font size: Choose the product title font size: Extra large, Large, Medium, Small, or Extra small.
  • Quantity: Allows customers to adjust the number of product units they want to purchase before adding to cart.
    • Width: Set the width of the quantity block as a percentage of the available row width (e.g., 33%). Blocks appear side by side until the row is full. Narrow blocks widen on tablets, and every block takes full width on mobile.
    • Show divider on the right: Enable to display a vertical divider on the right side of the block when blocks are placed side by side on desktop.
    • Content position: Align the quantity content to the Left, Center, or Right.
    • Placement: Choose where to display the quantity block: within Product details, in the Details box, left column, or in the Details box, right column.
  • Buy buttons: Displays the product purchase buttons, allowing customers to add products to their cart or proceed to checkout.
    • Width: Set the width of the buy buttons block as a percentage of the available row width (e.g., 33%). Blocks appear side by side until the row is full. Narrow blocks widen on tablets, and every block takes full width on mobile.
    • Show divider on the right: Enable to display a vertical divider on the right side of the block when blocks are placed side by side on desktop.
    • Placement: Choose where to display the buy buttons block: within Product details, in the Details box, left column, or in the Details box, right column.
    • Show dynamic checkout button: Enable to display the customer's preferred payment method, such as PayPal or Apple Pay, as a Buy now button.
    • Show gift card recipient form: Enable to let customers send gift cards to someone else by entering the recipient's details. Buy it now buttons are hidden on gift cards when this option is enabled.
  • Variant picker: Allows customers to select product variants, such as size, color, or other available options.
    • Content position: Align the variant picker content to the Left, Center, or Right.
    • Placement: Choose where to display the variant picker: in the Details box, left column, Details box, right column, or within Product details.
    • Picker style: Choose how product variants are displayed: Buttons, Dropdowns, or Color as buttons, others as dropdowns.
    • Show variant image on color swatches: Enable to display the variant image instead of the swatch color. This applies to options named Color and any names added under Theme settings > Swatches > Extra swatch option names.
    • Swatch size: Choose the size of the color swatches: Small, Medium, or Large. Medium and Large sizes display the image alongside the option name.
    • Show price: Enable to display the price alongside the variant options.
  • Description: Displays the product description on the product page.
    • Placement: Choose where to display the Description: in the Details box, left column, Details box, right column, or within Product details.
    • Heading: Enter an optional heading displayed with the product description (e.g., Description).
    • Heading layout: Choose how the heading is positioned relative to the description: Beside the description or Above the description.
    • Read more:
      • Shorten long descriptions: Toggle on to display a read more button when the product description exceeds the specified number of lines.
      • Lines to show: Set the number of description lines visible before the text is shortened (slider, e.g., 4).
      • Read more button text: Enter custom text displayed when the description is shortened (e.g., Read more).
      • Read less button text: Enter custom text displayed when the full description is expanded (e.g., Read less).
  • Heading and description: Displays a customizable heading and rich-text description on the product page.
    • Placement: Choose where the block appears: Product details or Details box, right column.
    • Heading: Enter the heading text displayed with the description (e.g., Additional Details).
    • Heading layout: Choose how the heading is positioned relative to the description: Beside the description or Above the description.
    • Description: Add supporting content using the rich-text editor. You can format text, add links, create bulleted or numbered lists, and connect dynamic sources.
  • Complementary products: Displays related or recommended cross-sell items that complement the current product, encouraging customers to explore additional products and increasing order value.
    • Placement: Choose where the complementary products block appears: Product details or Details box, right column.
    • Heading: Set the title displayed with the complementary products (e.g., Pairs well with).
    • Heading layout: Choose how the heading is positioned relative to the products: Beside the products or Above the products.
    • Products to show: Set the maximum number of complementary products displayed in the section (slider, e.g., 10).
    • Image ratio: Choose the display aspect ratio for gallery images (Portrait, Square, Landscape, or Adapt to image).
    • Show sale and sold out badges: Toggle on to display sale and sold-out badges on the relevant product cards.
    • Show quick view button on image: Toggle on to display a quick view button on product images. Products with options open in quick view, while products without options are added directly to the cart.

    How to Add Complementary Products

    Steps
    • Go to your Shopify Admin and navigate to Apps.
    • Open the Search & Discovery app.
    • Click on the Recommendations tab in the left sidebar.
    • Select the product for which you want to configure complementary recommendations (e.g., Apexline GT).
    • In the Edit complementary products card, click Browse or search to select the paired items.
    • Click Save to apply your complementary recommendations.
  • SKU: Displays the product’s Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) on the product page. This unique identifier helps store owners and customers track and reference specific products efficiently.
    • Width: Set the width of the SKU block (e.g., 100%). Blocks can sit side by side until a row is full. Narrow blocks widen on tablets, and every block occupies full width on mobile.
    • Show divider on the right: Toggle on to display a vertical divider on the right side of the block when blocks are positioned side by side on desktop.
    • Content position: Choose the horizontal alignment of the SKU content: Left, Center, or Right.
    • Placement: Choose where the complementary products block appears: Product details Details box, left column or Details box, right column.
    • Heading: Enter an optional heading displayed with the SKU (e.g., SKU). Leave empty to show no heading.
    • Heading layout: Choose how the heading is positioned relative to the SKU: Beside the SKU or Above the SKU.
  • Separator: Adds a visual separator between product information blocks to improve the layout and organization of the product details area.
    • Opacity: Adjust the transparency of the separator line (slider, e.g., 15).
    • Width: Set the width of the separator block (e.g., 100%). Blocks sit side by side until a row is full. Narrow blocks widen on tablets, and every block occupies full width on mobile.
    • Show divider on the right: Toggle on to display a vertical divider on the right side of the block when blocks are positioned side by side on desktop.
    • Placement: Choose where the separator appears: Product details, Details box, left column, or Details box, right column.
  • Size chart: Adds a size chart button to the product page, allowing customers to view size guides and choose the correct product size.
    • Width: Set the width of the size chart block (e.g., 100%). Blocks sit side by side until a row is full. Narrow blocks widen on tablets, and every block occupies full width on mobile.
    • Show divider on the right: Toggle on to display a vertical divider on the right side of the block when blocks are positioned side by side on desktop.
    • Content position: Choose the horizontal alignment of the size chart content: Left, Center, or Right.
    • Placement: Choose where the separator appears: Product details, Details box, left column, or Details box, right column.
    • Button text: Enter the text displayed on the size chart button (e.g., Size chart). Supports dynamic source connections.
    • Button icon: Select the icon displayed alongside the button text: None, Ruler, or Measuring tape.
    • Size chart image: Select or upload an image containing the size chart information for desktop devices. Supports dynamic source connections.
    • Size chart image on mobile: Select or upload a separate size chart image for mobile devices. This setting is optional; the desktop image is used when no mobile image is selected.
    • Or show a page: Select a Shopify page containing your size chart information. This page is displayed when no size chart image is set. Supports dynamic source connections.
    • Only show for products with a specific option: Toggle on to display the size chart only for products that have a specific product option.
  • Share buttons: Displays social sharing buttons on the product page, allowing customers to share the product with others.
    • Content position: Choose the horizontal alignment of the share buttons: Left, Center, or Right.
    • Placement: Choose where the separator appears: Product details or Details box, right column.
    • Heading: Enter an optional heading displayed with the share buttons (e.g., Social Share). Leave empty to hide the heading. Supports dynamic source connections.
    • Heading layout: Choose how the heading is positioned relative to the share buttons: Beside the share buttons or Above the share buttons.
  • Payment icons: Displays accepted payment provider icons on the product page, helping customers quickly identify available payment options and build trust during the purchase process.
    • Content position: Choose the horizontal alignment of the payment icons: Left, Center, or Right.
    • Placement: Choose where the separator appears: Product details or Details box, right column.
    • Heading: Enter an optional heading displayed with the share buttons (e.g., Social Share). Leave empty to hide the heading. Supports dynamic source connections.
    • Heading layout: Choose how the heading is positioned relative to the payment icons: Beside the icons or Above the icons.
  • Store pickup: Displays real-time local pickup availability for physical store locations. When local pickup is activated in your Shopify admin (Settings → Shipping and delivery → Local pickup), this block dynamically informs customers whether the product is in stock at a nearby store, expected collection readiness time (e.g., Usually ready in 24 hours), and store address details, allowing shoppers to choose in-store pickup over delivery.
    • Placement: Choose where the separator appears: Product details or Details box, right column.
    • Heading: Enter an optional heading displayed with the Pickup availability information (e.g., Local Pickup). Leave empty to hide the heading. Supports dynamic source connections.
    • Heading layout: Choose how the heading is positioned relative to the payment icons: Beside the pickup details or Above the pickup details.
  • Icon features: Displays up to three feature highlights on the product page. Each item includes an icon, heading, and description to communicate important product benefits, services, or information.
    • Placement: Choose where the icon features block appears (e.g., Details box, right column).
    • Show heading: Toggle on to display the section heading. When disabled with the Beside layout, the heading space is retained so the feature items align with other blocks.
    • Heading layout: Choose how the section heading is positioned relative to the feature items (e.g., Beside the items).

    Feature Items

    The Icon features block includes three individually configurable items. Each item has the following settings:

    • Icon: Select an icon to represent the feature (e.g., Box or Shield).
    • Custom icon: Upload a custom SVG image to replace the selected icon.
    • Heading: Enter the title of the feature (e.g., Easy returns). Supports dynamic source connections.
    • Description: Enter a short explanation of the feature (e.g., Simple return process). Supports dynamic source connections.

Related Products

The Related Products section displays products that are similar or connected to the product customers are currently viewing. This feature helps customers discover additional items, encourages product exploration, and increases opportunities for cross-selling. The section automatically loads related products based on the current product and displays them in a product card layout.

How to Configure Related Products

  • Color scheme: Choose a color scheme to control the background, text, and accent colors of the Related Products section. Use Edit scheme to customize the selected scheme.
  • Heading: Set the title displayed above the product list (e.g., Similar Games). The text editor supports bold, italic, and links, and the heading can be connected to a dynamic source.
  • Highlight italic words: Select the words to highlight in the heading and press the italic (I) button. Those words are shown upright with the theme’s gradient.
  • Heading font size: Choose the font size of the section heading: Extra large, Large, Medium, Small, or Extra small.
  • Heading alignment: Align the heading to the left, center, or right.
  • Description: Add supporting text displayed below the section heading. The text editor supports bold, italic, links, and bulleted or numbered lists.
  • Description width: Set the width of the description using the slider (e.g., 490 px).
  • Maximum products to show: Control how many related products are shown in total (slider, e.g., 8).
  • Show navigation arrows: Turn this option on to show navigation arrows on the product slider.

Product Cards Settings

  • Card style: Choose the appearance of the product cards: Expanding panel or Standard.
  • Image ratio: Choose the display aspect ratio for gallery images (Portrait, Square, or Adapt to image).
  • Show second image on hover: Toggle on to display an alternate product image when customers hover over a product card, giving them another view of the product.
  • Show vendor: Toggle on to display the product vendor or brand name on the product cards.
  • Show sale and sold out badges: Toggle on to display sale and sold out badges on the product cards.
  • Show stock status: Toggle on to display the stock status of the product on the card.
  • Show description: Toggle on to display a short product description on the card.
  • Description lines: Set how many lines of the description are shown (slider, e.g., 2).
  • Show color swatch: Toggle on to display color swatches on the product cards.
  • Show quick add: Toggle on to display a quick add button on the product cards.
  • Quick add button style: Choose the style of the quick add button: Primary or Secondary.
  • Products per row on mobile: Choose how many products appear in each row on mobile devices: 1 column or 2 columns.

Section Spacing Settings

  • Edge shadows: Softly blend the top and bottom edges into the sections around this one. Pick the color scheme used by the section above or below.
    • Show top shadow: Toggle on to display a shadow along the top edge.
    • Show bottom shadow: Toggle on to display a shadow along the bottom edge.
    • Bottom shadow color scheme: Select the color scheme for the bottom shadow.
    • Bottom shadow height: Adjust the height of the bottom shadow (slider, e.g., 120 px).
  • Desktop padding: Adjust the spacing around the Related Products section on desktop devices:
    • Padding top: Controls empty space above the section (slider, e.g., 0 px).
    • Padding bottom: Controls empty space below the section (slider, e.g., 70 px).
  • Mobile padding: Adjust the spacing around the Related Products section on mobile devices:
    • Padding top: Controls top spacing on mobile screens (slider, e.g., 40 px).
    • Padding bottom: Controls bottom spacing on mobile screens (slider, e.g., 40 px).

How to Add Related Products

Steps
  • Go to your Shopify Admin and open the Products section.
  • Select the product for which you want to manage related products.
  • Open the Search & Discovery app from your Shopify Admin.
  • Navigate to the Recommendations section.
  • Select the products you want to display as related products for the selected product.
  • Add the selected products as related product recommendations.
  • Click Save to apply the related product settings.
