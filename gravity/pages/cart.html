Theme documentation · v3.2

Cart

In the Gravity theme, the Cart serves as a key area where customers can review and manage their selected products before proceeding to checkout. It provides an organized summary showing product details, quantities, and prices for easy management.

Designed for a seamless shopping experience, the cart lets customers adjust quantities, remove items, apply discounts, or add special instructions with ease—helping smooth the path to purchase.

Cart setup

Configuring your cart

The Gravity theme offers flexibility in how the cart appears on your store. You can choose between a Cart Drawer—a slide-out panel—or a dedicated Cart Page that displays all cart details on its own page.

Steps
  • From Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes.
  • Click Customize on your active Gravity theme.
  • Open Theme Settings from the sidebar.
  • Select the Cart & Cart Drawer options.
  • Choose your preferred cart display type: Drawer or Page.
  • Save your changes.

Options

Cart display options

  • Cart Drawer: A panel that slides out from the side, allowing customers to view and modify their cart without leaving the current page.
  • Cart Page: Redirects shoppers to a full page dedicated to reviewing cart contents before moving to checkout.

Additional cart features

  • Display Tax Information: Show tax details in the cart so customers can see applicable taxes upfront.
  • Enable Cart Notes: Let customers add special instructions or requests with their order directly from the cart.
  • Allow Discount Codes: Show a field where customers can enter promo codes before checking out.

Activating extra cart options

Step
  • Go to Online Store → Themes in your Shopify admin.
  • Open the Customize tool on the Gravity theme.
  • Navigate to Theme Settings → Cart & Cart Drawer.
  • Toggle on the options you want like Tax Info, Cart Notes, or Discount Input.
  • Save your changes to apply them.
