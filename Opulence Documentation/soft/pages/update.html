What is a Theme Update?

A theme update involves upgrading your Shopify store’s theme to the latest version. Updates for the Soft theme are released regularly to introduce new features, enhance performance, and improve overall functionality and design.

How to Update the Theme

At Soft, we frequently release updates to introduce new features, boost performance, and enhance both the functionality and design of your store. While we are currently working on the latest version of the Soft theme, we recommend reviewing Shopify guide on updating themes. Once the new version is ready, the guide will help you smoothly integrate the latest improvements to keep your store performing at its best.
