Featured Collection

The Featured Collection section allows you to showcase a curated set of products from a selected collection directly on the homepage. Use it to highlight trending or promotional items.

Configure display type, number of products, columns and product card behavior such as quick view and secondary images.

Configure featured collection Try the theme

Setup

Featured Collection Settings

The Featured Collection section provides several customization options to control how products appear and how customers interact with them.

  • Color Scheme – Choose the color scheme for the section to match your store's design.
  • Heading – Add a section heading such as “Best Selling Products” to highlight featured items.
  • Select Collection – Choose the collection to display products from.
  • Products to Show – Control how many products to display.
  • Columns per Row – Set how many product cards appear per row on desktop.
  • Enable Slider – Turn on to show products in a horizontal slider; otherwise a grid is used.
  • Show View All Button – Enable a link/button to the full collection page.
  • Product Card Options – Configure vendor, secondary image on hover, quick view, and swatches.
  • Section Spacing – Adjust top and bottom spacing for desktop and mobile.

Product Card

Product Card Settings

Customize individual product cards: image aspect ratio, title transform, vendor display, and quick actions.

  • Show Vendor – Display the product vendor on the card.
  • Image Aspect Ratio – Choose square, portrait, or landscape ratios.
  • Show Secondary Image on Hover – Toggle hover image behavior.
  • Enable Quick View – Allow customers to preview product details.

Steps

How to configure the featured collection

Steps

  • Open the Theme Customizer.
  • Navigate to the Featured Collection section on the homepage.
  • Select the collection and set products to show, layout, and columns.
  • Configure product card options like quick view and secondary image.
  • Enable View All if you want a link to the full collection page.
  • Click Save to apply the changes.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

You can find the Featured Collection section in the Theme Customizer. From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Themes and click Customize. In the homepage template, locate the Featured Collection section to manage product display and layout settings.

Select the collection, set the number of products and layout, then click Save.
