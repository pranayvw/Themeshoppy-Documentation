How to update Gravity

A theme update keeps your Shopify store aligned with the latest product improvements, design quality, and feature updates while preserving your storefront setup.

Read update guide Shopify guide

Overview

What is a theme update?

A theme update involves upgrading your Shopify store’s theme to the latest version. Updates for the Gravity theme are released regularly to introduce new features, enhance performance, and improve overall functionality and design.

How to update the theme

At Gravity, we frequently release updates to introduce new features, boost performance, and enhance both the functionality and design of your store. While we are currently working on the latest version of the Gravity theme, we recommend reviewing the Shopify guide on updating themes. Once the new version is ready, the guide will help you smoothly integrate the latest improvements to keep your store performing at its best.
