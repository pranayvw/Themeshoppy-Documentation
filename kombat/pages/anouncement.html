Announcement Bar

The Announcement Bar appears at the top of your store and helps you share important updates, promotions, new game releases, and gaming deals with your customers.

In the Kombat theme, you can choose between marquee and slider display types, customize the scrolling direction and speed, show language and country/region selectors, and adjust edge shadows and section spacing.

Configure announcement Try the theme

Setup

Steps to Set Up the Announcement Bar in Kombat

  • From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes.
  • Click Customize on the Kombat theme.
  • In the Theme Editor, select the Announcement Bar section from the left sidebar.
  • Add announcement blocks, choose a display type, and customize the available settings according to your needs.
  • Click Save to apply your changes.

Follow these steps to configure your Announcement Bar

  • Color scheme: Choose a color scheme to control the background and text colors of the announcement bar.
  • Display type: Choose Marquee to continuously scroll announcement messages or Slider to display them as slides. The slider is displayed only when multiple announcement blocks are added.
  • Marquee direction: Choose whether the announcement messages scroll from left to right or right to left. This setting is available when the Marquee display type is selected.
  • Marquee speed: Set how long the marquee takes to complete its scrolling cycle, in seconds. This setting is available when the Marquee display type is selected.
  • Show language selector: Enable this option to display the language selector in the announcement bar.
  • Show country/region selector: Enable this option to display the country and region selector in the announcement bar.
  • Edge shadows: Enable the top or bottom shadow to blend the announcement bar smoothly into the sections around it. You can choose a color scheme for the bottom shadow and adjust its height.
  • Top spacing: Adjust the space above the announcement bar section.
  • Bottom spacing: Adjust the space below the announcement bar section.
  • Custom CSS: Add custom CSS to make additional styling adjustments to the section.

Available Blocks

  • Announcement block: Add individual announcement blocks to display custom promotional messages in the bar.
    • Desktop text: Enter the announcement message to display on desktop screens (for example: Level up your setup with premium gaming gear, limited-time discounts, and lightning-fast worldwide delivery today.).
    • Mobile text: Enter a shorter version of the announcement message for mobile screens (for example: Limited-time gaming deals and fast worldwide delivery.).
