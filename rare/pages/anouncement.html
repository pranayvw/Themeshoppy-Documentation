Announcement Bar

The Announcement Bar is displayed at the top of your store and is designed to highlight important messages for your customers. It is commonly used to promote special offers, shipping updates, sales announcements, or important store information.

In the Rare theme, the Announcement Bar is highly customizable. You can add multiple announcement messages, control how they appear, adjust their scrolling or sliding behavior, and customize the layout to match your store design.

Configure announcement Try the theme

Setup

Steps to Set Up the Announcement Bar in Your Shopify Theme, Rare

  • From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes.
  • Click Customize on the Rare theme.
  • In the Theme Editor, select the Announcement Bar section from the left sidebar.
  • Add announcement blocks and adjust the available settings according to your needs.
  • Click Save to apply your changes.

Follow these steps to configure your Announcement Bar

  • Color scheme: Choose a color scheme to control the background and text colors of the announcement bar section.
  • Enable autoplay: Turn this option on to automatically rotate announcement slides.
  • Rotate slides after every: Set the time duration in seconds for how often announcement slides change (for example, 4).
  • Enable currency/region: Enable this option to display the currency and region selector dropdown in the announcement bar.
  • Enable language: Enable this option to display the language selector dropdown in the announcement bar.
  • Top spacing: Adjust the spacing above the announcement bar section (for example, 8).
  • Bottom spacing: Adjust the spacing below the announcement bar section (for example, 8).

Available Blocks

  • Announcement block: Add individual announcement blocks to display custom promotional messages in the bar.
    • Desktop text: Enter the announcement text message to display on desktop screens (for example: Free shipping, certified craftsmanship and timeless jewelry designed to shine every single day.).
    • Mobile text: Enter a shorter, optimized announcement message for mobile screens (for example: Free shipping & easy returns).

Questions

Frequently asked questions

You can add multiple announcement blocks in the Announcement Bar section. These messages can be displayed either as a scrolling marquee or as rotating slides depending on the selected mode.

Ensure that the Slider Mode is selected and that Autoplay is enabled. If multiple announcement blocks are added, they will automatically rotate based on the configured slide duration.
