Testimonial

The Testimonial section highlights customer feedback, press mentions, and curated product highlights in an elegant, interactive curved carousel arc. Customers can click and browse through rotatable photo cards, accompanied by a spotlight area below displaying a decorative gemstone icon, narrative quote, and reviewer name.

Testimonial Section Settings

Configure section-wide settings such as the color scheme, heading hierarchy, narrative description, and desktop/mobile spacing.

Steps

  • From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes.
  • Click Customize to open the Theme Editor.
  • In the template sidebar, select Testimonial (or click Add section → Testimonial).
  • Choose a Color scheme for the section background and typography.
  • Enter your section Heading, choose a Font size, and add narrative Description copy.
  • Adjust Desktop spacing and Mobile spacing sliders for optimal padding.
  • Click Save to apply your changes.
  • Color scheme: Select a predefined theme color palette (e.g., Scheme 1, Scheme 2, etc.) to set the background and text colors for the section. Click Edit scheme to customize colors directly.
  • Heading: Enter the main section headline displayed at the top (for example: CURATED HIGHLIGHTS). Supports dynamic source connections.
  • Font size: Select the heading scale level (e.g., H1, H2, H3, H4, H5) to control typography hierarchy.
  • Description: Enter supporting narrative text explaining the highlights (for example: Discover authentic highlights showcasing the quality, beauty, and lasting impressions of our jewelry collections.). Supports rich text formatting (bold, italic, links) and dynamic source connections.
  • Desktop spacing:
    • Padding top: Adjust the top vertical spacing on desktop screens (in pixels, e.g., 40 px).
    • Padding bottom: Adjust the bottom vertical spacing on desktop screens (in pixels, e.g., 0 px).
  • Mobile spacing:
    • Padding top: Adjust the top vertical spacing on mobile screens (in pixels, e.g., 30 px).
    • Padding bottom: Adjust the bottom vertical spacing on mobile screens (in pixels, e.g., 30 px).

Testimonial Block Settings

  • Testimonial: Each block represents an individual review or highlight card in the curved carousel arc.
    • Image: Upload or select the primary photo displayed on the top curved carousel card (e.g., customer portrait, model shot, or jewelry piece photo).
    • Icon: Upload or select an optional decorative graphic or gemstone icon displayed in the spotlight area directly above the testimonial quote.
    • Description: Enter the customer review or quote narrative copy (for example: DISCOVER THE CAPTIVATING BEAUTY OF GEMSTONES THROUGH RICH, NATURAL COLOURS, UNIQUE FORMS, AND EXQUISITE CRAFTSMANSHIP...).
    • Customer name: Enter the reviewer or customer name. Supports dynamic source connections.

FAQs

Where can I find and add the Testimonial section?

From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Themes and click Customize. In the template sidebar, locate Testimonial or click Add section > Testimonial to add it to any page template.

How does the curved carousel arc work?

The section automatically arranges all added Testimonial image cards along a 3D curved arc. Clicking on any card smoothly rotates the arc, bringing the selected card to the center and instantly displaying its matching decorative icon, testimonial quote, and customer name below.

Can I customize the gemstone icon and card photo for each testimonial?

Yes. Each Testimonial block contains dedicated Image and Icon image pickers, allowing you to showcase unique model photos and gemstone or emblem illustrations for every review.

How many testimonial blocks can I add?

You can add multiple Testimonial blocks by clicking Add Testimonial in the left sidebar. We recommend adding 5 to 7 testimonials for an optimal, balanced curved carousel presentation.
