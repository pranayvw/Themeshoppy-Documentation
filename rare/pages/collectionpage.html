Collection Page

The Collection Page in the Rare theme displays products from your catalog in a refined, high-converting grid layout. It empowers customers to browse collections seamlessly with interactive filtering, customizable sorting controls, dynamic live swatches, quick view popups, and flexible column configurations.

How to Customize the Collection Page

Follow these step-by-step instructions in the Shopify Theme Editor to access and configure the Collection template:

Steps

  • From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes.
  • Click Customize next to the Rare theme.
  • From the top template selector dropdown, select Collections → Default collection.
  • In the template section list on the left, click on the Collection section.
  • Configure your Header color scheme, Heading alignment, Show collection image, and Show description toggles.
  • Adjust the Product columns (e.g., 5) and Products per page (e.g., 15) sliders.
  • Set up Filters (Enable sorting, Sort style, Expand filter groups) and Product cards (Aspect ratio, Secondary image on hover, Quick view, Variant swatches).
  • Adjust Desktop padding and Mobile padding sliders and click Save.

Collection Section Settings

The Collection section settings panel gives you complete control over headers, product columns, filtering mechanisms, card features, and spacing:

  • Header settings:
    • Header color scheme: Choose a dedicated palette (e.g., Scheme 1, Scheme 2) to style the collection title and header area. Click Edit scheme for live adjustments.
    • Show collection image: Toggle on to display the featured collection banner image at the top of the page.
    • Heading alignment: Select the horizontal alignment for the collection title (choose Left, Center, or Right; set to Left).
    • Show description: Toggle on to display the collection description text below the title.
  • Grid layout:
    • Color scheme: Select a color palette for the main collection product grid background and card text surfaces.
    • Product columns: Set the number of product cards displayed per row on desktop screens (slider from 1 to 5, e.g., 5).
    • Products per page: Set the maximum number of products displayed per pagination page (slider, e.g., 15).
  • Filters:
    • Enable sorting: Toggle on to show the sorting selector (e.g., Featured, Best Selling, Price low to high, Date) allowing shoppers to reorder results.
    • Sort style: Choose how sorting choices are presented (choose Radio buttons or Dropdown; set to Radio buttons).
    • Expand filter groups by default: Toggle on to display all filter accordions (Price, Availability, Color, Material, etc.) expanded open when visitors open the filter panel.
  • Product cards:
    • Image aspect ratio: Select the aspect ratio for product photography (choose Square, Portrait, Landscape, or Adapt to image; set to Square).
    • Show secondary image on hover: Toggle on to reveal the second product photo when customers hover their mouse over a product card.
    • Show vendor: Toggle on to display the brand/vendor name above product titles.
    • Enable quick view: Toggle on to display the quick view modal button for instant product previewing and checkout.
    • Show variant swatches: Toggle on to display clickable color and material swatches directly on collection product cards for interactive previewing.
  • Desktop padding:
    • Padding top: Set top vertical spacing on desktop viewports (slider in pixels, e.g., 10 px).
    • Padding bottom: Set bottom vertical spacing on desktop viewports (slider in pixels, e.g., 40 px).
  • Mobile padding:
    • Padding top: Set top vertical spacing on mobile screens (slider in pixels, e.g., 10 px).
    • Padding bottom: Set bottom vertical spacing on mobile screens (slider in pixels, e.g., 40 px).

How to Customize Collection Filters Using Search & Discovery

Filters displayed in the Filter & Sort drawer/panel are powered by Shopify's official Search & Discovery app. This app lets you customize filters by product attributes such as Price, Availability, Brand, Product Type, and custom Metafield swatches:

Filter Setup Steps

  • From your Shopify Admin, go to Apps → Search & Discovery.
  • Click Filters in the app navigation menu.
  • Click Edit filters or Add filter to select attributes (e.g., Price, Availability, Product type, Color, or Custom Metafields).
  • Reorder or customize display names as desired and click Save.

For more details, refer to Shopify Search & Discovery Documentation.

FAQs

How do I access and customize the Collection template?

In your Shopify Theme Editor, click the page selector dropdown at the top center of the screen, navigate to Collections > Default collection, and select the Collection section in the left sidebar.

How do I configure product filtering and sorting options?

Enable the Enable sorting toggle in the Collection section settings. To customize the filters (such as price, availability, or material), install and configure Shopify's free Search & Discovery app.

How do I enable variant color swatches on collection product cards?

In the Collection section settings under Product cards, enable the Show variant swatches toggle. Color options will automatically render as interactive swatches below the product imagery.

Why are some products not appearing on the collection page?

Ensure that the product status is set to Active in your Shopify admin, assigned to the specific collection, and published to the Online Store sales channel.
