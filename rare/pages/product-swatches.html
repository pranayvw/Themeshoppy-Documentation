Theme documentation

Product Color Swatches

The Product Color Swatches in the Rare theme allow customers to preview and select available product colors visually on product pages, collection grids, and featured product blocks. Utilizing Shopify's native Category Metafields ensures seamless, automated swatch rendering with custom hex colors, images, and shapes.

Configure product swatches Try the theme

Setup

Product Color Swatches

1. Assign a Product Category

Shopify requires products to have an assigned category in order to unlock standard category metafields such as Color, Size, and Material.

Steps

  • From your Shopify admin, navigate to Products.
  • Select the product you want to configure.
  • Under the Category field, select or browse for the appropriate product category (for example: Bracelets in Jewelry).
  • Click Save to confirm the assigned category.

2. Configure the Color Category Metafield

Once a category is assigned, Shopify automatically enables standard category metafield attributes for that product.

Steps

  • Scroll down to the Category metafields card on the product page.
  • Locate the Color attribute.
  • Select or accept suggested color entries (e.g. Green, Gold, Silver, Blue), or add custom color values.
  • Click Save.

3. Connect Dynamic Source to Variant Options

Link your product's variant options to the category metafield dynamic source so Shopify feeds color and swatch data directly to the storefront.

Steps

  • In the Variants section of the product page, locate your Color option.
  • Click the Connect dynamic source icon (database icon) in the top-right of the option box.
  • Select the Color category metafield as the dynamic source.
  • Verify that each variant entry is linked to its corresponding color value.
  • Click Save.

4. Customize Swatch Shape in Rare Theme Settings

Configure global visual styling for color swatches across your entire store through the Theme Editor.

Steps

  • From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes.
  • Click Customize next to the Rare theme.
  • Click on Theme Settings (gear icon) in the left sidebar.
  • Select the Color swatches section.
  • Choose your preferred Swatch shape: Pills, Circle, or Square.
  • Under Swatch option names, specify any comma-separated variant option names (e.g. color, colour, material, finish) to trigger swatches.
  • Click Save to apply your changes.
Read more about adding color swatches using category metafields in Shopify: Shopify Category Metafields Guide

Questions

Frequently asked questions

To use Shopify's native visual color swatches with hex codes and custom images, products must have an assigned product category with the corresponding category metafields linked.

Go to Theme Settings → Color swatches in the Theme Editor and select either Pills, Circle, or Square. The chosen shape will update across all product cards and product pages.

In Theme Settings → Color swatches, enter your option names in the Swatch option names field (comma-separated, e.g., color, colour, shade, material, finish). The theme will automatically render them as visual swatches.
