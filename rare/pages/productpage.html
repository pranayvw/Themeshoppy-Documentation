Product Page

The Product Page is one of the most important pages in your Shopify store, as it presents a single product along with all the essential information customers need before making a purchase. In the Rare theme, the product page is designed to highlight your product with a clean layout, high-quality media, and well-structured details that help customers easily understand the product and make confident buying decisions.

This page includes a product media gallery, product title, vendor name, pricing details, SKU, and a detailed product description. Customers can also select product variants such as size or color, check inventory availability, adjust the quantity, and add the item to their cart directly from this page. Additional blocks such as pickup availability, trust badges, complementary products, collapsible information tabs, and social share buttons can be enabled to enhance the shopping experience and provide more useful information to your customers.

How to Configure the Product Page

Steps

  • From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes.
  • Click Customize on the Rare theme.
  • In the Theme Editor, use the page selector dropdown and choose Products → Default product.
  • From the left sidebar, select the Product section to access the product page settings.
  • Add, remove, or rearrange product blocks such as Title, Price, Variant Picker, Inventory, and Add to Cart according to your store’s needs.
  • Adjust the section settings and layout options available on the right panel.
  • Click Save to apply your changes.

Product Section Settings

The Product section settings control the overall visual styling, product media gallery layout, slider navigation arrows, split details arrangement, and responsive spacing for the product page template.

  • Color scheme: Choose from predefined theme color palettes (e.g., Scheme 1, Scheme 2, etc.) to style section background surfaces, card containers, and typography. Click Edit scheme to customize colors directly.
  • Image gallery:
    • Media type: Select how product imagery and videos are displayed on the product page. You can choose between Side way and Slider:
      • Side way: Arranges the product media gallery and product details side-by-side in a split two-column layout. The product imagery is displayed in a dedicated column alongside product information (title, price, variant swatches, and purchase buttons), allowing customers to view images and options simultaneously.
      • Slider: Displays product media in a horizontal slider/carousel positioned across the top of the product section. Customers can navigate through multiple product images using previous/next slider arrows, with product information and purchase buttons placed beneath the carousel.
    • Media size: Select how product media fits within the viewport container (choose Contain to show complete images without cropping, or Cover to fill the gallery area).
  • Slider preview arrow:
    • Background: Choose the background color for the previous media navigation arrow (e.g., #000000).
    • Label: Choose the arrow icon color (e.g., #FFFFFF).
    • Hover background: Choose the background color when hovering over the arrow (e.g., #F7F7F7).
    • Hover label: Choose the arrow icon color when hovered (e.g., #000000).
  • Slider next arrow:
    • Background: Choose the background color for the next media navigation arrow (e.g., #000000).
    • Label: Choose the arrow icon color (e.g., #FFFFFF).
    • Hover background: Choose the background color when hovering over the arrow (e.g., #F7F7F7).
    • Hover label: Choose the arrow icon color when hovered (e.g., #000000).
  • Product details:
    • Split details: Toggle on to split product information into distinct left and right columns. The left column showcases the badge, product headline, description, and variant swatches, while the right column features pricing, quantity controls, Add to Cart / Buy Now buttons, and collapsible detail accordions.
  • Desktop padding:
    • Padding top: Adjust top vertical spacing on desktop screens (slider in pixels, e.g., 20 px).
    • Padding bottom: Adjust bottom vertical spacing on desktop screens (slider in pixels, e.g., 40 px).
  • Mobile padding:
    • Padding top: Adjust top vertical spacing on mobile devices (slider in pixels, e.g., 20 px).
    • Padding bottom: Adjust bottom vertical spacing on mobile devices (slider in pixels, e.g., 40 px).

Available Product Block

  • Inventory: Displays real-time stock availability and inventory urgency badges on the product page (e.g., 9 LEFT, IN STOCK, SOLDOUT).
    • Block position: Choose whether the inventory status block is placed in the Left column or Right column of the product details area.
  • Title: Displays the product title prominently on the product page.
    • Block position: Choose whether the product title is placed in the Left column or Right column of the product details area.
    • Font size: Select the typography scale for the headline (select from H1, H2, H3, H4, or H5; set to H3).
  • Description: Displays the product narrative and detailed description on the product page.
    • Block position: Choose whether the description block is placed in the Left column or Right column of the product details area.
    • Read more:
      • Enable read more / read less: Toggle on to collapse long product descriptions and display an expandable toggle.
      • Lines to show when collapsed: Set the number of description lines visible before truncation (slider, e.g., 2).
      • "Read more" text: Enter custom label text for the expansion trigger (e.g., Read more).
      • "Read less" text: Enter custom label text for the collapse trigger (e.g., Read less).
  • Price: Displays the product’s pricing information on the product page, including the current price, compare-at price, and savings / sale badges.
    • Block position: Choose whether the price block is placed in the Left column or Right column of the product details area.
  • Vendor: Displays the product's brand or supplier name on the product page, helping shoppers quickly identify the manufacturer or designer associated with the product.
    • Block position: Choose whether the vendor block is placed in the Left column or Right column of the product details area.
  • SKU: Displays the product’s Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) on the product page. This unique identifier helps both store owners and customers track and reference specific products efficiently.
    • Block position: Choose whether the SKU block is placed in the Left column or Right column of the product details area.
  • Text: Allows you to add custom text, subtitle notes, badges, or short promotional copy anywhere in the product details area.
    • Text: Enter custom copy (e.g., Add small descriptive text). Supports rich text formatting such as bold, italic, and links, as well as dynamic source connections.
    • Block position: Choose whether the text block is placed in the Left column or Right column of the product details area.
  • Separator: Adds a subtle horizontal dividing line between blocks on the product page. This helps organize content and improve visual hierarchy by cleanly separating different elements.
    • Block position: Choose whether the separator line is placed in the Left column or Right column of the product details area.
  • Variant: Allows customers to choose between different product options such as size, color, material, or finish.
    • Block position: Choose whether the variant block is placed in the Left column or Right column of the product details area.
    • Options type: Select how product variants are displayed (choose Dropdown or Button; set to Button).
    • Enable variant image on swatch: Toggle on to display product variant thumbnail images directly inside the swatch circles.
    • Option text: Select the text label color for inactive option buttons (e.g., #000000).
    • Active option color: Select the highlight/border accent color for the active selected variant option (e.g., #B6303A).
    • Opacity: Adjust the background fill opacity percentage for option buttons ( slider, e.g., 15 %).
    To know more about how to set up color swatches: Click here.
  • Size chart: Displays an interactive sizing guide button that opens a popup modal window, helping shoppers select the perfect size or dimensions before purchasing.
    • Block position: Choose whether the size chart button is placed in the Left column or Right column of the product details area.
    • Title: Enter the button trigger label (e.g., Size chart). Supports dynamic source connections.
    • Size chart icon: Upload or select an icon image to display alongside the size chart button label (e.g., image-40.png).
    • Desktop image: Upload a size guide chart or visual diagram image for desktop screens.
    • Mobile image: Upload an optional mobile-optimized sizing diagram for smaller screen viewports.
    • Select page: Choose an existing Shopify store page (such as a dedicated Size Guide page) to load inside the modal.
    • Link to a product option: Toggle on to conditionally display the size chart only when a specific product option is present.
    • Product option name: Enter the specific variant option name (e.g., Size, Dimensions, or Ring Size). If left blank, the size chart remains visible for all products.
  • Quantity: Allows customers to adjust the number of product units they want to purchase before adding to cart.
    • Block position: Choose whether the quantity selector is placed in the Left column or Right column of the product details area.
    • Background: Choose the background fill color for the quantity selector box (e.g., #FFFFFF).
    • Text color: Choose the text label and plus/minus icon color (e.g., #000000).
  • Buy now: Renders the primary purchasing buttons (such as Add to Cart and Buy It Now) allowing customers to purchase items easily.
    • Block position: Choose whether the buy now buttons are placed in the Left column or Right column of the product details area.
    • Dynamic checkout button: Toggle on to display express / accelerated checkout buttons (e.g., Buy It Now, Shop Pay, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay) so customers can bypass the cart drawer and check out immediately.
    • Gift card recipient form: Toggle on to enable gift card customization fields (recipient email, name, optional gift message, and scheduled delivery date) for digital gift card products, allowing shoppers to send gift cards directly to friends or family.
  • Collapsible tab: Displays collapsible content drawers (such as Fit Notes, Composition & Care, Lifetime Warranty) that allow customers to expand and view supplementary product information.
    • Block position: Choose whether the collapsible tab is placed in the Left column or Right column of the product details area.
    • Heading: Enter the tab title (e.g., FIT NOTES). Supports dynamic source connections.
    • Content: Add detailed description or rich text information displayed inside the expandable drawer. Supports rich text formatting (bold, italic, links, lists) and dynamic sources.
    • Default open: Toggle on to keep this tab open and expanded by default when the product page loads.
  • Icon block: Displays trust badges and highlight features with icons (e.g., PREMIUM QUALITY, EASY RETURNS, FAST & FREE DELIVERY, STYLE SUPPORT) to build customer confidence during shopping.
    • Block position: Choose whether the icon block is placed in the Left column or Right column of the product details area.
  • Complementary products: Displays related or recommended cross-sell items (e.g., paired jewellery pieces) that complement the current product, encouraging higher order values.
    • Block position: Choose whether the complementary products block is placed in the Left column or Right column of the product details area.
    • Heading: Set the title displayed above the complementary products list (e.g., PAIRED WITH). Supports dynamic source connections.
    • Number of products to load: Controls how many complementary products are loaded in total (slider, e.g., 6).
    • Image aspect ratio: Select the shape and proportions of the product images displayed in the cards (e.g., Square).

    How to Add Complementary Products

    Steps

    • Go to your Shopify Admin and navigate to Apps.
    • Open the Search & Discovery app.
    • Click on the Recommendations tab in the left sidebar.
    • Select the product for which you want to configure complementary recommendations (e.g., INFINITE CURVE EARRINGS).
    • In the Edit complementary products card, click Browse or search to select the paired items.
    • Click Save to apply your complementary recommendations.
  • Share buttons: Displays social sharing icons on the product page, allowing customers to easily share the product across supported social media platforms.
    • Block position: Choose whether the share buttons block is placed in the Left column or Right column of the product details area.
  • Payment icons: Displays accepted payment provider icons on the product page, helping customers quickly identify available payment options and build trust during the purchase process.
    • Block position: Choose whether the payment icons block is placed in the Left column or Right column of the product details area.
  • Store pickup: Displays real-time local pickup availability for physical store locations. When local pickup is activated in your Shopify admin (Settings → Shipping and delivery → Local pickup), this block dynamically informs customers whether the product is in stock at a nearby store, expected collection readiness time (e.g., Usually ready in 24 hours), and store address details, allowing shoppers to choose in-store pickup over delivery.
    • Block position: Choose whether the store pickup block is placed in the Left column or Right column of the product details area.

Related Products

The Related Products section displays products that are similar or connected to the product customers are currently viewing. This feature helps customers discover additional items, encourages product exploration, and increases opportunities for cross-selling. The section automatically loads related products based on the current product and displays them in a product card layout.

How to Configure Related Products

  • Color scheme: Choose a color scheme (such as Scheme 1) to control the background, text, and accent colors of the Related Products section.
  • Heading: Set the title displayed above the product list (e.g., SIMILAR PRODUCTS). Supports rich text formatting and dynamic source connections.
  • Font size: Select the typography scale for the section heading (select from H1, H2, H3, H4, or H5; set to H4).
  • Description: Add supporting subtitle or descriptive text displayed below or alongside the section heading. Supports rich text formatting and dynamic sources.
  • Number of products to load: Control how many related products are fetched in total (slider, e.g., 10 col).
  • Number of columns per row: Define how many product cards appear in a single row on desktop devices (slider, e.g., 5 col).
Product Cards Settings
  • Image aspect ratio: Select the shape of product images displayed inside product cards (choose from Square, Portrait, or Adapt to image; set to Square).
  • Show secondary image on hover: Toggle on to display an alternate product image when customers hover over a product card, giving them another view of the product.
  • Show vendor: Toggle on to display the product vendor or brand name on product cards.
Section Spacing Settings
  • Desktop padding: Adjust the spacing around the Related Products section on desktop devices:
    • Padding top: Controls empty space above the section (slider, e.g., 40 px).
    • Padding bottom: Controls empty space below the section (slider, e.g., 97 px).
  • Mobile padding: Adjust the spacing around the Related Products section on mobile devices:
    • Padding top: Controls top spacing on mobile screens (slider, e.g., 40 px).
    • Padding bottom: Controls bottom spacing on mobile screens (slider, e.g., 100 px).

How to Add Related Products

Steps

  • Go to your Shopify Admin and open the Products section.
  • Select the product for which you want to manage related products.
  • Open the Search & Discovery app from your Shopify Admin.
  • Navigate to the Recommendations section.
  • Select the products you want to display as related products for the selected product.
  • Add the selected products as related product recommendations.
  • Click Save to apply the related product settings.

FAQs

Why are my product images not displaying correctly?

This usually happens when the image media size setting does not match the uploaded image proportions. Try switching Media size between Contain and Cover in the product section settings, or upload high-resolution images with consistent aspect ratios for the best presentation.

What is the "Split details" feature and how does it work?

Split details divides your product information into a modern 2-column layout. When enabled under the Product section settings, each product block can be assigned to either the Left column or Right column using its individual Block position setting.

How do I set up color swatches for product variants?

Under the Variant block, set Options type to Button and enable Enable variant image on swatch if you wish to display mini variant thumbnails. To configure custom colors and pattern images, check out our comprehensive Product Swatches Guide.

How do I show the Size chart only for products with specific variant options?

In the Size chart block settings, toggle on Link to a product option and enter the exact name of your variant option (e.g., Size or Ring Size). The size guide trigger button will then only be visible on products that include that option.
