Cart

The Cart in the Rare theme is designed to optimize conversion rates and streamline the checkout flow. It allows shoppers to review their selected items, adjust quantities, apply promotional discount codes, add special order notes, and track real-time free shipping goals.

Rare provides high flexibility with support for both a dynamic Cart Drawer (slide-out side panel) and a full dedicated Cart Page template.

Cart Theme Settings

Global cart settings control cart behavior, display format (Drawer vs. Page), free shipping targets, terms agreement checkboxes, and cross-sell recommendations when the cart is empty:

Steps to Configure Cart Theme Settings

  • From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes and click Customize.
  • In the Theme Editor, click the Theme settings (gear icon) in the left sidebar.
  • Click to expand the Cart section.
  • Choose your preferred Cart type (Drawer or Page).
  • Configure options for Show cart note, Show tax info, Show discount option, and Show free shipping bar.
  • Set up Terms and conditions and Empty cart recommendations as desired.
  • Click Save to apply your changes.
  • Cart type: Choose how the cart displays when shoppers add products or click the cart icon:
    • Drawer: A sleek slide-out panel opens from the side of the screen, allowing customers to review and update items without navigating away from the current page.
    • Page: Navigates shoppers directly to the dedicated /cart template page.
  • Show cart note: Toggle on to display an expandable special instructions / order notes textarea for customer requests or gift messages.
  • Show tax info: Toggle on to display tax and shipping calculation notices below the cart total.
  • Show discount option: Toggle on to display a promotional coupon/discount code field with an Apply button directly in the cart drawer and cart page.
  • Show free shipping bar: Toggle on to display an animated progress bar that calculates how much more customers need to spend to unlock free shipping (e.g., Spend $4,750.00 more for Free Shipping).
  • Free shipping threshold: Enter the minimum cart amount required to unlock free shipping (e.g., 5000).
  • Terms and conditions:
    • Require terms agreement: Toggle on to require shoppers to tick a mandatory checkbox before the checkout button becomes active.
    • Agreement text: Enter custom rich text message (e.g., I agree with the Terms & conditions).
    • Terms page: Select or link your store's terms of service or privacy policy page. Opens in a new tab when clicked.
  • Empty cart recommendations:
    • Show products when cart is empty: Toggle on to show product suggestions when a shopper opens an empty cart.
    • Heading: Enter headline text for suggestions (e.g., You may also like).
    • Source: Select whether recommendations are pulled from a Collection or custom product list.
    • Collection: Select the catalog collection (e.g., Anniversary) to feature when the cart is empty.

Cart Page Section Settings

When Cart type is set to Page (or when visitors view the /cart URL), you can customize the dedicated Cart Page section layout, color schemes, and viewport spacing:

Steps to Customize Cart Page Template

  • From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes → Customize.
  • From the top template selector dropdown, select Cart.
  • Click the Cart section in the left sidebar.
  • Set the primary Color scheme for the cart items table.
  • Under Side bar, select the dedicated Color scheme for the Order Summary card.
  • Configure Desktop padding and Mobile padding sliders and click Save.
  • Color scheme: Select a color palette (e.g., Scheme 1, Scheme 2) to style the main cart items table background, table headers (Product, Price, Quantity, Total Price), and text. Click Edit scheme for live color tweaks.
  • Side bar Color scheme: Select a dedicated color palette specifically applied to the right-hand ORDER SUMMARY box, total pricing, discount code area, and checkout buttons.
  • Desktop padding:
    • Padding top: Adjust top vertical spacing on desktop screens (slider in pixels, e.g., 40 px).
    • Padding bottom: Adjust bottom vertical spacing on desktop screens (slider in pixels, e.g., 40 px).
  • Mobile padding:
    • Padding top: Adjust top vertical spacing on mobile screens (slider in pixels, e.g., 40 px).
    • Padding bottom: Adjust bottom vertical spacing on mobile screens (slider in pixels, e.g., 40 px).

FAQs

How do I switch between the Cart Drawer and dedicated Cart Page?

In your Shopify Theme Editor, open Theme settings (gear icon) > Cart. Under Cart type, select Drawer for a slide-out panel or Page to direct customers to the full cart template.

How does the Free Shipping progress bar work?

Enable Show free shipping bar and enter your target amount in the Free shipping threshold field. The cart automatically tracks the subtotal and displays dynamic countdown messages encouraging shoppers to add more items to qualify.

How do I require customers to accept Terms & Conditions before checkout?

In Theme settings > Cart, toggle on Require terms agreement. Customers must check the terms box before the checkout button unlocks. You can also link directly to your store's policy page in the Terms page field.

How do I show product recommendations when the cart is empty?

Under Empty cart recommendations in Theme settings > Cart, enable Show products when cart is empty and select your preferred collection in the Collection picker.
