Shop the look

The Shop the look section provides an interactive visual merchandising experience that allows shoppers to discover and purchase complete styled ensembles or jewellery sets directly from a single editorial photograph. Pinned hotspot markers across the main lifestyle image correspond to specific products, revealing live pricing, inventory badges, and quick-view options as customers interact with them.

Shop the look Section Settings

Configure section-wide options including color scheme, lifestyle look image and aspect ratio, typography, decorative accent rules, hotspot pin styling, product card features, and desktop/mobile spacing.

Steps

  • From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes.
  • Click Customize to open the Theme Editor.
  • In the template sidebar, select Shop the look (or click Add section → Shop the look).
  • Choose a Color scheme and upload your editorial lifestyle photo under Look image.
  • Configure Content settings including the Heading, Font size, narrative Description, and Accent rule.
  • Customize Hotspot appearance (pin size, background color, and active ring color).
  • Set up Product card display preferences such as aspect ratio, secondary hover image, and quick view.
  • Click + Add Hotspot in the left sidebar to add individual product pins and position them across the look image.
  • Click Save to publish your section.
  • Color scheme: Select a predefined theme color palette (e.g., Scheme 1, Scheme 2, etc.) to set the background and text styling for the section. Click Edit scheme to customize colors directly.
  • Look image:
    • Image: Upload the main high-resolution editorial lifestyle or model photograph.
    • Image ratio: Select the aspect ratio for the lifestyle look image (e.g., Adapt to image, Square, Portrait, or Landscape).
  • Content:
    • Heading: Enter the main headline displayed above the product showcase (for example: SHOP THE LOOK). Supports dynamic source connections.
    • Font size: Select the heading scale level (e.g., H1, H2, H3, H4, H5) to control title typography size.
    • Description: Enter supporting narrative copy (for example: Discover genuine customer experiences celebrating timeless craftsmanship and exceptional quality.). Supports rich text formatting (bold, italic, links) and dynamic source connections.
    • Show accent rule under heading: Toggle on to display an elegant decorative accent underline bar directly below the section heading.
    • Accent rule color: Choose a custom color for the accent underline bar (for example: #B23A4B).
    • Text alignment: Select the alignment for the heading and description copy (Left or Center).
  • Hotspot appearance:
    • Hotspot size: Adjust the pixel diameter of the interactive hotspot markers using the slider (e.g., 26 px).
    • Hotspot background: Choose the background fill color for the hotspot pin buttons (e.g., #FFFFFF).
    • Hotspot icon and active ring: Choose the color for the inner + / - icon and the active pulsing focus ring (e.g., #B6303A).
  • Product card:
    • Card image aspect ratio: Select the shape and aspect ratio for the displayed product card photo (e.g., Square, Portrait, or Adapt to image).
    • Show vendor: Toggle on to display the product brand or manufacturer name.
    • Show secondary image on hover: Toggle on to reveal the second product image when customers hover over the product card.
    • Show color swatches: Toggle on to display color and material swatches directly on the product card.
    • Show quick view button: Toggle on to allow shoppers to open an instant Quick View modal without leaving the page.
  • Desktop spacing:
    • Padding top: Adjust the top vertical spacing on desktop screens (in pixels, e.g., 60 px).
    • Padding bottom: Adjust the bottom vertical spacing on desktop screens (in pixels, e.g., 60 px).
  • Mobile spacing:
    • Padding top: Adjust the top vertical spacing on mobile screens (in pixels, e.g., 40 px).
    • Padding bottom: Adjust the bottom vertical spacing on mobile screens (in pixels, e.g., 40 px).

Hotspot Block Settings

  • Hotspot: Each hotspot block places an interactive clickable pin marker over the main lifestyle image, connecting directly to a catalog product.
    • Product: Select the specific product from your Shopify catalog that corresponds to this hotspot pin (e.g., INFINITE CURVE EARRINGS).
    • Horizontal position: Adjust the horizontal X-axis placement percentage across the look image using the slider (from 0% on the left edge to 100% on the right edge, e.g., 34%).
    • Vertical position: Adjust the vertical Y-axis placement percentage down the look image using the slider (from 0% at the top edge to 100% at the bottom edge, e.g., 38%).
    • Preview image: Upload an optional custom thumbnail image displayed when hovering over the hotspot marker. Defaults to the selected product's featured photo if left empty.

FAQs

Where can I find and add the Shop the look section?

From your Shopify admin, navigate to Online Store > Themes and click Customize. In the template sidebar, locate Shop the look or click Add section > Shop the look to add it to your homepage or custom page template.

How do I accurately position the hotspot pins on my lifestyle image?

Under each Hotspot block, use the Horizontal position and Vertical position percentage sliders to position the marker pin with precision over the exact jewellery or accessory item shown in the lifestyle image.

What happens when a customer clicks or hovers on a hotspot pin?

Clicking a hotspot pin instantly highlights that item's product card in the adjacent showcase area, displaying its current price, inventory status badge, product title, and Quick View option for effortless cart additions.

Can I add multiple hotspots to a single lifestyle image?

Yes. You can click + Add Hotspot in the Theme Editor sidebar to add multiple pins for different pieces featured in the image (such as earrings, necklaces, bracelets, and rings).
