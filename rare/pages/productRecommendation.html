Product recommendation

The Product recommendation section delivers an interactive, guided Piece Finder / Gift Finder quiz experience directly on your storefront (such as PIECE FINDER – Explore personalized jewelry recommendations). It helps shoppers discover products tailored to their exact preferences by answering visual, multi-step questions (e.g., choosing metal type, jewelry style, or occasion) and receiving live, curated product recommendations.

How It Works

The Product recommendation feature operates as a smart, two-column interactive experience that connects customer choices on the left with curated product recommendations on the right:

  • 1. Multi-Step Quiz (Left Side): Shoppers are presented with intuitive question cards (e.g., 01 WHICH METAL SPEAKS TO YOU?). Each question contains up to 4 rich visual options featuring thumbnail photos, titles (Gold, Silver, Rose Gold, Platinum), and descriptive subtitles. Shoppers can click the arrow buttons (← / →) or slide indicators to move between questions.
  • 2. Real-Time Dynamic Matching (Right Side): When a shopper clicks any option card, the recommendation panel instantly updates without reloading the page. It highlights matching items under the customizable Recommended for you badge.
  • 3. Rich Product Details & Badges: Recommended product cards display high-resolution imagery, dynamic availability badges (such as NEW | INSTOCK, SOLDOUT, POPULAR | 9 LEFT, or LIMITED EDITION | 1 LEFT), product titles, regular and sale pricing, and variant swatches.
  • 4. Smart Fallback Handling: If a customer's specific combination of answers does not yield an assigned product, the recommendation box automatically displays a friendly, customizable fallback message (No products match your selection. Please choose the proper options to see recommendations.).

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

Follow these step-by-step instructions in your Shopify Theme Customizer to set up and configure the piece finder quiz:

Setup Steps

  • Step 1: Open Theme Customizer — From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes and click Customize.
  • Step 2: Add the Section — In the template sidebar, click Add section and select Product recommendation.
  • Step 3: Configure Header & Description — In the section settings panel, select a Color scheme, enter the Heading (e.g., PIECE FINDER), set the Font size (e.g., H5), and enter the intro Description text.
  • Step 4: Set Recommendation Labels — Under Recommendations panel, enter the badge title (e.g., Recommended for you) and define the fallback No match message.
  • Step 5: Create Question Blocks — Click + Add Questions under the section in the left sidebar (you can add up to 3 question steps).
  • Step 6: Fill Question & Option Details — In each Question block, enter the Question headline (e.g., WHICH METAL SPEAKS TO YOU?) and configure the 4 answer options with Option title, Option description, and upload a thumbnail Option image.
  • Step 7: Assign Products & Mapping Rules — Under Recommended products, choose the Product to recommend and fill in the Mapping field with your answer combinations (e.g., 1+1+1, 3+1+2, 1+3+2) where each number represents the selected option per question.
  • Step 8: Adjust Spacing & Save — Set your preferred Desktop padding and Mobile padding sliders and click Save to publish your quiz.

Product recommendation Section Settings

The Product recommendation section settings panel gives you complete control over colors, header typography, the recommendation panel, product mapping combinations, carousel behavior, viewport spacing, and custom styling:

  • Color scheme: Select a predefined theme color palette (e.g., Scheme 1, Scheme 2, etc.) to control background color, question card surfaces, typography, and accent borders. Click Edit scheme to customize colors directly.
  • Heading: Enter the primary section headline displayed on the left side of the split header (for example: PIECE FINDER). Supports rich formatting (bold, italic, links) and dynamic source connections.
  • Font size: Select the typography scale for the section heading (choose from H1, H2, H3, H4, H5, or H6; set to H5 in the screenshot).
  • Description: Enter supporting narrative copy displayed to the right of the red accent bar (for example: Explore personalized jewelry recommendations curated to help you find your next favorite piece with confidence.). Supports rich text formatting (bold, italic, links) and dynamic sources.
  • Recommendations panel:
    • Recommendations label: Enter the badge title text displayed above the recommended product card (for example: Recommended for you).
    • No match message: Enter fallback rich text displayed when a customer's selected combination has no assigned products (for example: No products match your selection. Please choose the proper options to see recommendations.).
  • Recommended products (Product Mapping):

    The theme includes a powerful mapping engine that allows you to link specific catalog products to unique quiz answer combinations chosen by your shoppers. Under each Select product entry, configure the following fields:

    • Product: Select the catalog product from your Shopify store (for example, Rose Gold Overlapping Loop Ring) that should be displayed in the recommendation panel when a shopper's quiz answers match.
    • Mapping: Enter the combination formula string (e.g., 1+1+1, 3+1+2, 1+3+2, 3+1+1).

      How the Mapping Syntax Works:

      • Each number represents the selected option index (1, 2, 3, or 4) for each respective question, joined by a plus sign (+).
      • Formula Structure: [Question 1 Option] + [Question 2 Option] + [Question 3 Option]
      • Example: 1+2+3 means: Question 1 → Option 1 (e.g., Gold), Question 2 → Option 2 (e.g., Minimalist), and Question 3 → Option 3 (e.g., Rings).
      • Multiple Combinations (Comma Separated): You can assign multiple quiz paths to the same product by separating them with commas (for example: 1+1+1, 3+1+2, 1+3+2, 3+1+1, 3+2+1). When a shopper selects any of these combination paths, the assigned product will immediately appear in the recommendation box.
  • Results layout:
    • Image ratio: Select the aspect ratio for recommended product images (Adapt to image, Square, Portrait, or Landscape).
    • Show secondary image on hover: Toggle on to reveal the product's alternate hover image when a shopper hovers their cursor over the card.
    • Show vendor: Toggle on to display the brand/vendor name above the product title.
    • Enable quick view: Toggle on to display a quick view button that lets shoppers preview details and add items to cart directly from a modal popup.
    • Show pagination dots: Toggle on to display navigation slide dots below the recommended products slider.
  • Desktop padding:
    • Padding top: Adjust top vertical spacing on desktop screens (in pixels, e.g., 40 px).
    • Padding bottom: Adjust bottom vertical spacing on desktop screens (in pixels, e.g., 40 px).
  • Mobile padding:
    • Padding top: Adjust top vertical spacing on mobile screens (in pixels, e.g., 30 px).
    • Padding bottom: Adjust bottom vertical spacing on mobile screens (in pixels, e.g., 30 px).

Questions Block Settings

Each Questions block represents an individual step in the guided recommendation quiz. You can add up to 3 question blocks in total to tailor the customer journey.

  • Question: Enter the primary question title displayed at the top of the step (for example: WHICH METAL SPEAKS TO YOU?). Supports dynamic source connections.
  • Options (Option 1 to 4): Each question supports up to four distinct answer options:
    • Option: Enter the choice name or title (for example: Gold, Silver, Rose Gold, or Platinum). Supports dynamic source connections.
    • Option description: Enter supporting descriptive text explaining the material, style, or benefit (for example: Timeless luxury with warmth, brilliance, and lasting elegance.). Supports dynamic source connections.
    • Option image: Upload a representative thumbnail image for the option tile (e.g., Rectangle_45230.png).

FAQs

Where can I find and add the Product recommendation section?

From your Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Themes and click Customize. In the template sidebar, locate Product recommendation or click Add section > Product recommendation to add the guided piece finder quiz to your homepage, product page, or custom landing pages.

How does the Mapping field work to recommend products?

The Mapping field uses option indices formatted as 1+2+3, where each number corresponds to the answer chosen for Question 1, Question 2, and Question 3. You can list multiple combination paths separated by commas (e.g., 1+1+1, 3+1+2, 1+3+2) so that whenever a customer picks any of those combinations, the selected product is dynamically shown.

How many question blocks and options can I create?

You can add up to 3 Questions blocks (e.g., Question 1: Metal, Question 2: Style, Question 3: Price/Occasion). Each question block supports up to 4 individual options with custom titles, descriptions, and thumbnail photos.

What is displayed if no products match a customer's selection?

If no products match a specific combination, the section elegantly displays the customizable No match message (e.g., No products match your selection. Please choose the proper options to see recommendations.).
